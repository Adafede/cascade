    cascade 0.0.0.9002

    Architecture & Performance Overhaul

    • Reduced external package dependencies from 17 to 7 (baseline, curl, data.table, ggplot2, gt, mzR, plotly), removing BiocParallel, caTools, forcats, htmltools, httr2, MSnbase, purrr, R.utils, tidytable, and tima.
    • Consolidated 66 fragmented R/*.R files into 20 cohesive modules with explicit Collate ordering and full Roxygen2 documentation.
    • Rewrote all tabular pipelines in idiomatic data.table (fread(), fwrite(), foverlaps(), rbindlist(), melt(), dcast()) and base R vectorization.
    • Replaced MSnbase::chromatogram() with a vectorized S4 MS1 scan extractor (extract_ms_peak(), extract_ms_progress()) that parses and binary-searches MS1 scans across all features simultaneously.
    • Replaced caTools::runmean() with a fast base-R centered moving average (runmean_center()) and MSnbase::closest() with a vectorized binary-search matcher (closest_idx()).

    Completed In-Progress Functions & Palettes

    • Acetonitrile gradient response correction (p_acn_i(), predict_response()): Vectorized both functions in R/annotations.R and wired them into prepare_comparison() and generate_pseudochromatograms() via correct_acn = FALSE, acn_time = 30.5, and dvol = 0.5.
    • Signal-based sunburst plots (plot_results_2()): Completed in R/hierarchy.R using data.table and wired into generate_pseudochromatograms(), returning plots_2 (sunburst_signal_based_duo and sunburst_conf_signal_based_duo) alongside plots_1 and treemaps.
    • Interactive Wikidata/LOTUS gt tables (format_gt()): Completed in R/query_wikidata.R without requiring htmltools and wired into generate_ids() via format_tables = FALSE (returning tables alongside plots, treemaps, and sunbursts).
    • Titled treemaps and sunbursts (treemaps_progress()): Completed in R/hierarchy.R with explicit tables argument passing to display unique structure counts in plot titles, and wired into generate_ids() via show_title = FALSE.
    • Color palettes (R/colors.R): Preserved all qualitative and sequential color palettes (microshades, microshades_grey, microshades_colors, paired, minibits, tableau20, discrete_rainbow_14, discrete_rainbow_23, elife, green_4, blue_4, pink_4, green_24, blue_24, pink_24) and bundled them into cascade_palettes.

    Retired Prototype Functions

    • plot_histograms(): Superseded by plot_histograms_confident() and plot_histograms_taxo() (for continuous retention-time pseudochromatograms) and plot_histograms_litt() (for discrete taxon/sample stacked bar charts).
    • plot_chromatogram(): Unused 15-line plotly scratch wrapper expecting a non-standard time column (cascade uses rtime); superseded by check_chromatograms_alignment() and plot_peak_detection().
    • prehistograms_progress() and histograms_progress(): Trivial 1-line map() wrappers around prepare_plot() and plot_histograms(), replaced by direct lapply() calls in generate_ids() and plot_tima().
    • save_histograms_progress() and save_treemaps_progress(): Referenced external global pipeline variables (paths, dimensions, size) not present in the package and relied on deprecated plotly::save_image().

    Ontology & Metadata

    • Verified and standardized all EDAM ontology terms (topic_3172, topic_0154, topic_3370, topic_2258, topic_0780, topic_0637, topic_0092, operation_3803, operation_3214, operation_3801, operation_3215, operation_3628, operation_3799, operation_3203, operation_2942, operation_3460, operation_3564, operation_0224, operation_0337, data_2536, data_0943, data_0962, data_1086, data_1463, data_1712, data_1872, format_3244, format_3475, format_3752, format_2331, format_3790, format_1196, format_1199) against official EDAM.tsv across DESCRIPTION, codemeta.json, CITATION.cff, inst/CITATION, and @concept tags in R/*.R.
